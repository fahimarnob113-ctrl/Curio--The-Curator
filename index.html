Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is CURIO really 100% free and open-source? ▼
Yes! CURIO is licensed under the permissive MIT License. There are no subscriptions, ads, or paywalls. You can use it personally, commercially, or modify the code freely.
Where are my uploaded images and data stored? ▼
Everything is stored directly on your computer's local hard drive inside your browser's dedicated IndexedDB database. No data or images are ever uploaded to an external server.
How do backups work? ▼
Clicking Export packages all your catalogs into a self-contained .zip archive containing structured JSON metadata and an /images/ folder with all your uploaded logos and gallery photos as real PNG/JPG files.
Can I run CURIO completely without internet? ▼
Yes! All icons, CSS, fonts, and the JSZip archiver are embedded directly inside the single HTML file. You can run it on an airplane, in an offline bunker, or from a USB flash drive.
