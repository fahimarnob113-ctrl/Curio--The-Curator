## Notable Cross-Category Figures (Controversial or Debated Legacies)
- **Christopher Columbus** — celebrated as an explorer, widely reassessed for colonization/atrocities against Indigenous peoples
- **Napoleon Bonaparte** — hailed as a modernizing legal reformer (Napoleonic Code) and condemned for wars costing millions of lives
- **Genghis Khan** — remembered as both a brutal conqueror and a unifier who enabled Silk Road trade and cultural exchange
- **Winston Churchill** — praised as a WWII wartime leader, criticized over colonial policy (notably the Bengal famine)
- **Mao Zedong** — founding figure of modern China, also linked to the Great Leap Forward famine and Cultural Revolution death tolls
- **Andrew Jackson / Thomas Jefferson** — reassessed in recent decades over slavery and Indigenous displacement policies
