## Notable Cross-Cultural Mythological Archetypes
- **Trickster figures** — Loki (Norse), Anansi (West African), Sun Wukong (Chinese), Coyote (Native American), Hermes (Greek), Eshu (Yoruba)
- **Flood myth figures** — Noah (Abrahamic), Utnapishtim (Mesopotamian), Manu (Hindu), Deucalion (Greek)
- **Dying-and-rising god motifs** — Osiris (Egyptian), Dionysus (Greek), Persephone (Greek), Baldr (Norse)
- **Sun deities** — Ra (Egyptian), Surya (Hindu), Amaterasu (Japanese), Inti (Incan), Apollo (Greek)
- **Creator/first-being figures** — Pangu (Chinese), Nüwa (Chinese), Izanagi/Izanami (Japanese), Brahma (Hindu), Viracocha (Incan)
