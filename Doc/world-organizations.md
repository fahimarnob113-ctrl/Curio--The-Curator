## Notable/Controversial Organizational Moments
- **League of Nations (1920–1946)** — the UN's failed predecessor, widely seen as ineffective at preventing WWII, dissolved after the UN's founding
- **WTO Doha Round collapse** — long-running global trade talks that never reached full agreement
- **WHO's COVID-19 response** — controversial early messaging and US withdrawal threats/actual 2020 withdrawal notice (later rescinded)
- **OPEC oil embargo (1973)** — reshaped global energy politics
- **Brexit (UK leaving the EU, 2016–2020)** — first major member departure from a regional economic union
- **ICC arrest warrants controversies** — repeated disputes over jurisdiction and enforcement against heads of state
