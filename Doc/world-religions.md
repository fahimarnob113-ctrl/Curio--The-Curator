# World Religions

Major religions and belief systems, grouped by origin/family, with core beliefs and practices noted.

---

## Abrahamic Religions
1. **Judaism** — monotheistic, oldest Abrahamic faith, Torah/Tanakh, covenant with God, Shabbat observance
2. **Christianity** — monotheistic, belief in Jesus Christ as savior, Bible (Old + New Testament); major branches: Catholicism, Eastern Orthodoxy, Protestantism
3. **Islam** — monotheistic, Muhammad as final prophet, Quran, Five Pillars; major branches: Sunni, Shia, Sufism
4. **Bahá'í Faith** — 19th-century origin, unity of all religions, founded by Bahá'u'lláh
5. **Druze** — esoteric offshoot with Islamic roots, closed community, reincarnation belief
6. **Samaritanism** — ancient offshoot of Israelite religion, distinct from mainstream Judaism
7. **Rastafari** — 20th-century Jamaican movement, Abrahamic-influenced, reveres Haile Selassie

## Dharmic (Indian-Origin) Religions
8. **Hinduism** — oldest major living religion, polytheistic/henotheistic, karma, dharma, reincarnation, Vedas/Upanishads
9. **Buddhism** — founded by Siddhartha Gautama, Four Noble Truths, Eightfold Path, no creator god; branches: Theravada, Mahayana, Vajrayana
10. **Jainism** — ahimsa (non-violence) central, founded/systematized by Mahavira, strict asceticism
11. **Sikhism** — founded by Guru Nanak, monotheistic, Guru Granth Sahib, Five Ks
12. **Ayyavazhi** — 19th-century Indian tradition centered on Ayya Vaikundar

## East Asian Religions & Philosophies
13. **Taoism (Daoism)** — founded/attributed to Laozi, the Tao, balance and naturalness, wu wei
14. **Confucianism** — founded by Confucius, ethics, social harmony, filial piety (often called a philosophy/social system rather than religion)
15. **Shinto** — indigenous Japanese religion, kami (spirits), nature worship, shrines
16. **Chinese Folk Religion** — blend of ancestor worship, Taoism, Buddhism, local deities
17. **Cao Dai** — 20th-century Vietnamese syncretic religion combining multiple traditions
18. **Yiguandao** — Chinese syncretic folk-religious movement

## Iranian / Zoroastrian Family
19. **Zoroastrianism** — one of the oldest monotheistic religions, founded by Zoroaster, Ahura Mazda, dualism of good/evil
20. **Yazidism** — syncretic Kurdish-region religion, Melek Taus (Peacock Angel) central
21. **Yarsanism (Ahl-e Haqq)** — Kurdish/Iranian syncretic tradition

## African Traditional & Diasporic Religions
22. **Yoruba Religion** — orishas (Ogun, Oshun, Shango, etc.), West African origin
23. **Vodou (Haitian)** — diasporic blend of West African religion and Catholicism
24. **Santería (Lucumí)** — Afro-Cuban blend of Yoruba religion and Catholicism
25. **Candomblé** — Afro-Brazilian religion, orisha veneration
26. **Akan Religion** — traditional Ghanaian belief system
27. **Kemetism** — modern revival of ancient Egyptian religion

## Indigenous & Native Religions
28. **Native American Religions** (diverse, tribe-specific) — animism, spirit world, ceremonies (varies widely: Lakota, Navajo, Hopi traditions, etc.)
29. **Australian Aboriginal Religion** — Dreamtime cosmology
30. **Andean/Inca-derived traditions** — Pachamama veneration, still practiced in parts of Peru/Bolivia
31. **Maori Religion** — Polynesian-origin, atua (gods/spirits), New Zealand
32. **Hawaiian Religion (traditional)** — akua (gods), kapu system

## European Pre-Christian & Revived Traditions
33. **Norse Paganism / Heathenry (modern revival: Ásatrú)** — Odin, Thor, Norse pantheon
34. **Celtic Polytheism (modern revival)** — Druidry, Celtic deities
35. **Slavic Paganism (modern revival: Rodnovery)** — Perun, Slavic pantheon
36. **Hellenism (modern revival)** — worship of the Greek pantheon
37. **Roman Polytheism (modern revival: Religio Romana)**

## New Religious Movements & Modern Movements
38. **Mormonism (Latter Day Saint movement)** — founded by Joseph Smith, Book of Mormon, Christian-derived
39. **Jehovah's Witnesses** — Christian-derived, distinct doctrine and practices
40. **Seventh-day Adventist Church** — Christian-derived, Saturday Sabbath
41. **Church of Scientology** — 20th-century founding by L. Ron Hubbard
42. **Unitarian Universalism** — liberal, non-creedal, pluralistic
43. **Falun Gong** — Chinese spiritual movement blending qigong and Buddhist/Taoist elements
44. **Wicca** — modern pagan witchcraft religion, founded mid-20th century
45. **Neo-Druidism** — modern nature-based spiritual movement
46. **Raelism** — UFO-based new religious movement
47. **Theosophy** — 19th-century syncretic esoteric movement

## Nontheistic / Philosophical Systems (often discussed alongside religion)
48. **Secular Humanism**
49. **Deism**
50. **Pantheism**
51. **Agnosticism** (position on belief, not a religion itself)
52. **Atheism** (absence of religious belief, not a religion itself)

## Notable Schisms & Branches
- **Christianity:** Great Schism (1054, Catholic vs. Orthodox), Protestant Reformation (1517, Luther)
- **Islam:** Sunni–Shia split (following the death of Muhammad, over succession)
- **Buddhism:** Theravada vs. Mahayana vs. Vajrayana divergence
- **Judaism:** Orthodox, Conservative, Reform, Reconstructionist movements
- **Hinduism:** Vaishnavism, Shaivism, Shaktism as major devotional traditions within the same broader religion
