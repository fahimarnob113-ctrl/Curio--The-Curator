# Animal Kingdom Classifications

Taxonomic structure of the animal kingdom, from broad classification levels down through major phyla, classes, and notable groups.

---

## Taxonomic Rank System (Broad to Specific)
1. Domain — Eukarya
2. Kingdom — Animalia
3. Phylum
4. Class
5. Order
6. Family
7. Genus
8. Species

---

## Major Animal Phyla

### Invertebrate Phyla
1. **Porifera** — sponges
2. **Cnidaria** — jellyfish, corals, sea anemones
3. **Ctenophora** — comb jellies
4. **Platyhelminthes** — flatworms (tapeworms, flukes)
5. **Nematoda** — roundworms
6. **Annelida** — segmented worms (earthworms, leeches)
7. **Mollusca** — snails, clams, octopuses, squid
8. **Arthropoda** — insects, arachnids, crustaceans, myriapods (largest animal phylum by species count)
9. **Echinodermata** — starfish, sea urchins, sea cucumbers
10. **Rotifera** — microscopic aquatic animals

### Chordate Phylum (includes all vertebrates)
11. **Chordata** — animals with a notochord/spinal structure at some life stage

---

## Classes Within Arthropoda
12. **Insecta** — insects (beetles, butterflies, ants, bees)
13. **Arachnida** — spiders, scorpions, ticks, mites
14. **Crustacea** — crabs, lobsters, shrimp, barnacles
15. **Myriapoda** — centipedes, millipedes

---

## Classes Within Chordata (Vertebrates)
16. **Agnatha** — jawless fish (lampreys, hagfish)
17. **Chondrichthyes** — cartilaginous fish (sharks, rays, skates)
18. **Osteichthyes** — bony fish (most fish species)
19. **Amphibia** — frogs, toads, salamanders, newts
20. **Reptilia** — snakes, lizards, turtles, crocodilians
21. **Aves** — birds
22. **Mammalia** — mammals

---

## Mammalian Subgroups/Orders (Notable)
23. **Primates** — humans, apes, monkeys, lemurs
24. **Carnivora** — cats, dogs, bears, seals
25. **Cetacea** — whales, dolphins, porpoises
26. **Chiroptera** — bats
27. **Rodentia** — rodents (largest mammal order)
28. **Artiodactyla** — even-toed ungulates (deer, cattle, pigs, giraffes, whales' land ancestors)
29. **Perissodactyla** — odd-toed ungulates (horses, rhinos, tapirs)
30. **Proboscidea** — elephants
31. **Marsupialia (infraclass)** — kangaroos, koalas, opossums
32. **Monotremata** — egg-laying mammals (platypus, echidna)
33. **Lagomorpha** — rabbits, hares, pikas
34. **Pinnipedia (informal group within Carnivora)** — seals, sea lions, walruses
35. **Xenarthra** — sloths, armadillos, anteaters

## Bird Orders (Notable)
36. **Passeriformes** — perching/songbirds (largest bird order)
37. **Accipitriformes** — eagles, hawks, vultures
38. **Strigiformes** — owls
39. **Anseriformes** — ducks, geese, swans
40. **Galliformes** — chickens, turkeys, pheasants
41. **Sphenisciformes** — penguins
42. **Struthioniformes** — ostriches (ratites)
43. **Psittaciformes** — parrots

## Reptile Orders (Notable)
44. **Squamata** — lizards and snakes
45. **Testudines** — turtles and tortoises
46. **Crocodilia** — crocodiles, alligators, caimans
47. **Rhynchocephalia** — tuatara (sole living member)

---

## Informal/Common Classification Groupings
- **Vertebrates vs. Invertebrates** — presence/absence of a backbone (invertebrates make up ~97% of all animal species)
- **Warm-blooded (endothermic) vs. Cold-blooded (ectothermic)** — birds/mammals vs. reptiles/amphibians/fish
- **Terrestrial, Aquatic, Amphibious, Aerial** — by primary habitat
- **Herbivore, Carnivore, Omnivore, Insectivore** — by diet

---

## Extinct/Historical Groupings Worth Noting
- **Dinosauria** — extinct archosaur clade, ancestors of modern birds
- **Trilobita** — extinct marine arthropods, common Paleozoic fossils
- **Ammonoidea** — extinct shelled cephalopods
- **Synapsida (non-mammalian)** — extinct mammal-like reptiles, ancestral line to mammals
